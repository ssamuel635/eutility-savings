Is your home typical?

500 kWh is a rounded average for an Executive flat. Yours depends on how many people live with you, how long the aircon runs and your appliances.

Your SP Group bill shows your actual usage under “Electricity”, in kWh. The average of your last few months gives you the best estimate.
