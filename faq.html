About Eutility

Who is Eutility?

Eutility is an independent energy company. It was founded in Australia in 1979 and is still family owned. We don’t sell electricity: we compare what the retailers offer and help Singapore homes switch to a plan that suits them.

Eutility Singapore Pte Ltd (UEN 201624008N) has had an office here since 2016, at 140 Paya Lebar Road, #05-08, AZ @ Paya Lebar, Singapore 409015.

Are you an electricity retailer?

No, and that’s the point. Eutility doesn’t generate or supply energy. Our job is independent advice: compare what the retailers are offering, backed by our own data analysis, and tell you which one suits you.

If your current plan is already the best one for your usage, we’ll say so. You stay with SP Group or whoever you’re with, and nothing changes. You always pay your retailer directly, never through us.

Does it cost anything to use Eutility?

Checking your savings is free, and there’s no obligation to switch. You always pay your retailer directly for your electricity.

What does “Get control” mean?

It’s Eutility’s promise to its clients: don’t overpay for energy, and know exactly what you’re paying for. For a home, that means knowing whether your plan still makes sense for how much you use, and having someone independent to check it.

Savings

How are my savings calculated?

We multiply your monthly usage (kWh) by each plan’s published rate, add any daily charge, and compare that with what you pay on your current plan. The difference × 12 is your yearly saving.

  • Discount-off-tariff plans use SP Group’s regulated tariff.
  • Time-of-use plans assume a share of your usage is off-peak (30% by default; you can change it under Advanced assumptions).
  • Sign-up promotions are shown separately and not added to your savings.

It’s an estimate. Your real bill depends on your actual usage and the retailer’s contract.

Which retailers can I compare?

We currently compare 32 residential plans from Geneco, Keppel Electric, PacificLight, Sembcorp Power, Senoko Energy, Tuas Power, against SP Group’s regulated tariff as the baseline.

SP Group revises its tariff every quarter, and we update it when it changes.

Where do I find my monthly usage?

On your electricity bill, look for the electricity usage in kWh for the month. If not, just send a photo of the bill to us via WhatsApp and we’ll read it for you!

Switching

How do I switch?

Tap Switch on WhatsApp or Switch by email on your results. That sends your details to our Singapore office: on WhatsApp, or by email to customerservice@eutility.com.sg if you’d rather not use WhatsApp. We confirm the plan with you, help you sign up with the retailer, and the retailer confirms your start date.

Nothing is submitted to a retailer without you agreeing to it first.

Will my electricity supply be interrupted?

No. SP Group continues to deliver electricity through the same grid and meter. Only the company you buy electricity from, and your price, changes.

What if I’m still in a contract?

Your current retailer may charge an early termination fee. We show each plan’s fee and deposit where the retailer publishes them, and can help you time the switch to avoid fees.

Is there a deposit?

Some retailers ask for a security deposit, usually set by your home type; others waive it or keep your existing SP Services deposit. Each plan’s deposit is shown with your results, under “Fees and deposit” in the full plan list.

Talking to us

On a computer without WhatsApp, scan the QR code with your phone instead.

I don’t use WhatsApp. Can I email instead?

Yes. Use Switch by email on your results, or Email us instead at the bottom of any page, and the same details are sent by email. You can also write to customerservice@eutility.com.sg any time, or call +65 9790 8347.

What happens to my bill photo?

We use it to read your usage and current plan so we can prepare your estimate and, if you ask, help you switch. See our Privacy Statement for how data is stored and protected.
