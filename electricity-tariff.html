How the tariff works.

  • SP Group reviews the tariff every quarter, for January, April, July and October, and the Energy Market Authority (EMA) approves it.
  • It follows the cost of the fuel used to generate electricity, so it can go up or down each quarter.
  • A fixed-price plan keeps the same rate for its whole contract, whatever happens to the tariff. A discount-off-tariff plan moves with the tariff.
  • You don’t have to stay on the tariff. Homes can buy electricity from a retailer instead, and SP Group still delivers it through the same wires and meter.
