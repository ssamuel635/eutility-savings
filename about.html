About Eutility

Independent energy experts since 1979.

Eutility is a family-owned, independent energy and utility management company. Since 1979 we’ve helped businesses lower their utility, carbon and telecommunications costs. Today that’s more than 2,000 businesses across 19,000+ locations, supported from Australia and our Singapore office. This site brings the same work to homes and small businesses: find out what you should be paying, and get help to move if it’s worth it.
