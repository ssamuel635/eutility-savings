How it works

Check, compare and switch, all in one WhatsApp chat.

Our WhatsApp assistant reads your bill, compares every plan and handles the switch. Mostly you just tap buttons. Prefer to do it yourself? The calculator here does the same comparison.

  1. Step 1

    Say hi on WhatsApp

    Send us any message, even just “Hi”, and tap Check My Saving. Coming from this website or our app? Your details are already in the message, so you skip straight to your results.

  2. Step 2

    Send a photo of your bill

    Snap your latest electricity bill and send it in the chat. The assistant reads your usage, your provider and your rate, then compares every plan for you.

    No bill handy? Tap I Don’t Have My Bill, then type roughly how much you use (in kWh or dollars) or tap Use Average Estimate, and pick your provider. We use your bill only to prepare your estimate and your switch. See our Privacy Statement.

  4. Step 4

    Switch with a tap

    Tap Switch Now and give your name, NRIC or FIN, and email. Your contract is prepared in the chat: tap Sign Contract and we submit the switch to your new retailer.

    You’ll get a confirmation on WhatsApp and by email within 1–2 working days, and your new rate starts from your next billing cycle. SP Group still delivers your power through the same wires and meter. Only the price changes.
