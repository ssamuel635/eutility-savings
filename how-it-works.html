How it works

Check, compare and switch, all in one WhatsApp chat.

Our WhatsApp assistant reads your bill, compares every plan and handles the switch. Mostly you just tap buttons. Prefer to do it yourself? The calculator here does the same comparison.

  1. Step 1

    Say hi on WhatsApp

    Send us any message and tap Check my savings. Coming from this website or our app? Your details are already in the message, so you skip straight to your results.

  2. Step 2

    Send a photo of your bill

    Snap your latest electricity bill and send it in the chat. The assistant reads your usage, your provider and your rate, and shows you each step as it works.

    No bill handy? Tap I don’t have my bill, type roughly how much you use, and pick your provider. We use your bill only to prepare your estimate and your switch. See our Privacy Statement.

  4. Step 4

    Switch with a tap

    Tap Switch now and give your name, NRIC or FIN, and email. Your contract is prepared in the chat for you to review and sign. We submit the switch to your new retailer.

    You’ll get a confirmation on WhatsApp and by email within 1–2 working days, and your new rate starts from your next billing cycle. SP Group still delivers your power through the same wires and meter. Only the price changes.
